\section{SIMULA}
\label{sec:simula}

SIMULA was one of several domain-specific languages of the time.
It is singled out because of its impact on programming languages and compilers,
but there were many similar (though less successful and impactful) languages
at the time.
More specifically, there were numerous languages designed specifically for simulation;
we do not cover all of their compilers here, largely because of the lack of their
overall impact relative to their number.
There may have been as many simulation languages as there were list- and string-processing
languages at the time, but only a few simulation languages had a lasting impact
and even fewer saw widespread use.
